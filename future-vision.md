# Future Vision

AI Career Mentor can evolve into a complete youth career preparation platform.

## Future Features
- Real CV parsing from uploaded files
- Gemini API integration for personalized analysis
- Interview simulation chatbot
- Company-specific preparation dashboard
- Personalized career roadmap
- Learning recommendations based on missing skills
- Rejection trend tracking
- Mentor support matching

## Long-Term Goal
To become an accessible digital mentor for young people who do not have equal access to career guidance.
