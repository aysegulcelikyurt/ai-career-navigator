# Social Impact

AI Career Mentor is designed as both a career technology tool and a social impact solution.

Many young people invest years in education, but they still lack access to mentorship, insider knowledge, and personalized preparation. This creates an invisible inequality in career development.

Some students know how to prepare for interviews, how to tailor their CVs, and how to understand company expectations. Others do not, even if they are equally talented.

This project aims to reduce that gap by making career preparation more accessible, structured, and personalized through AI.

Its social purpose is to help prevent young people’s talent, effort, and education from being wasted simply because they lacked guidance.
